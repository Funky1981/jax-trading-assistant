# WP-00.03C1E2A — v2 typed causal-attribution label sidecar freeze

## Decision and safety scope

C1E2A freezes typed expected-label sidecars and exact C1E3 scoring semantics
before any v5 model output exists. It does not execute C1E3, contact OpenAI or
another provider, load an experiment credential, generate inference, change
the v5 prompt/schema/policy/resolver, mutate either v2 dataset, mutate a
database, or perform any trading action. Phase 00 remains NO-GO.

C1E3 must be described as **CASE-BLIND / CATEGORY-AWARE INDEPENDENT HOLDOUT
EVALUATION**. C1E2 exposed aggregate v2 category distributions, but no
case-level EventInput, expected answer, issuer, proxy, or rationale. The
technical-lead disposition retains both v2 holdouts; neither was regenerated.

## Two-pass adjudication

Pass 1 used only frozen EventInput, the existing v2 mapping and rationale,
`event-asset-resolution-v1`, the accepted C1E1 architecture, and the frozen v5
role rubric. It included every issuer needed to distinguish principal, equal,
possible, secondary-affected, and context-only structure; it excluded terms
that were not issuer identities and exposures that were merely secondary or
contextual.

all v5 cardinality/coexistence invariants, exact normalized issuer uniqueness,
bounded proxy vocabulary, typed policy projection, and actual deterministic
resolver status. The validator reports contradictions and never repairs a
sidecar.

## C1E3 profile binding and provider isolation

The profiles remain:

- `openai-hosted-c1e3-generalization-v2/WP-00.03C1E3-GENERALIZATION`
- `openai-hosted-c1e3-boundary-v2/WP-00.03C1E3-BOUNDARY`

Each profile binds its exact sidecar path, identity, file hash, semantic
fingerprint, and the exact scoring rubric identity/hash/fingerprint. Missing,
swapped, or byte-modified material fails closed. Even with exact bindings,
C1E2A returns `C1E3 execution remains unauthorized`; this package cannot start
a provider request.

Provider isolation is structural: `V5InitialRequest` accepts only `EventInput`
and resolver-derived exposure vocabulary. Sidecars and scoring objects are not
parameters and are loaded only by offline/profile validation. Tests serialize
the actual strict provider wire and reject expected mapping/issuer/proxy/role,
candidate, rationale, adjudication, and scoring field names or values.

## Verification and remaining risk

The package-level sidecar, linkage, invariant, mapping consistency,
completeness, denominator, provider-isolation, profile-gate, and immutable-hash
tests pass. Full repository, vet, golden, direct nested golden, formatting,
diff, and credential scans are recorded in the final handover after execution.

Remaining semantic risk belongs to later authorized C1E3 scoring: Luna may omit
context/secondary issuers, overuse `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL`, or choose the wrong
principal proxy. Labels and denominators cannot be altered in response.
