## Reproduced historical evidence

The evidence below was recomputed from the three accepted C1B `report.json` artifacts and the original frozen 48-case manifest.

### DIRECT boundary

Across 144 evaluations:

- 75/75 correct DIRECT mappings had `market_relevance=HIGH`.
- 11/11 false DIRECT mappings had non-HIGH relevance: LOW or MEDIUM.
- No correct DIRECT mapping had LOW, MEDIUM, or UNCERTAIN relevance.

This supports the narrow in-sample condition `DIRECT AND market_relevance != HIGH` as an abstention trigger. It does not establish that all genuine causal issuer events must be HIGH relevance. A real causal issuer event with LOW or MEDIUM market relevance would be conservatively suppressed by this prototype. That is the principal out-of-sample risk and requires independent evaluation before promotion.

### PROXY / competing-issuer boundary

Across the same 144 evaluations:

- 18/18 correct PROXY mappings had no input entity recognized as a traded issuer by the active deterministic resolver.
- The two false PROXY mappings were repetitions of the same historical company-category event. Its structured `entities` input contained two distinct identities that exactly matched active traded-issuer identities in the versioned rules.
- The third repetition of that event was already UNRESOLVED.

The smallest condition supported by this evidence is therefore: `PROXY AND event_category=company AND at least two distinct input entities exactly match distinct active traded-issuer identities under the versioned receipt-time rules`. This identity count does not call ticker resolution or expose symbols. The rule does not inspect titles, summaries, model explanations, case IDs, expected labels, issuer-specific exceptions, or ticker tokens.

This condition deliberately does not abstain a broad non-company proxy merely because issuer entities are present. It also does not treat unknown entities as traded issuers. The evidence is small and in-sample; legitimate company-category events with multiple named issuers and a defensible principal broad proxy remain an out-of-sample false-abstention risk.

## Exact policy behavior

Reason codes:

- `DIRECT_RELEVANCE_NOT_HIGH`
- `PROXY_COMPETING_TRADED_ISSUERS`

Permitted transitions:

- DIRECT -> UNRESOLVED
- PROXY -> UNRESOLVED
- otherwise unchanged

The policy has no code path that creates a DIRECT or PROXY mapping, changes one issuer or exposure into another, or produces a ticker. The raw `StructuredResult` remains the canonical model output. `CausalConsistencyDecision.raw_mapping` and `effective_mapping` are separate values, and only the effective mapping is passed to `event-asset-resolution-v1`.
