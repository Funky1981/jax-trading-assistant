## C1F3 scoring freeze

The scoring freeze is `ai-shadow-causal-attribution-scoring-c1f3-v1`, file SHA-256 `7f795302a4c33c1a7c103294c1cab590a548c9e2cd746ca7908a10dc2a4a65a9`, semantic fingerprint `744a197191d2226005dd823b5793bafc86bf5e52164fd515efbe1efdc514162d`, bound to unchanged scoring source SHA-256 `f54ec91af32a9a11f3c0a7f27eefd3c52624d2360fce613820e7aa39380d8269`.

Two issuer/mapping lenses are mandatory:

- STRICT: historical deterministic string-canonicalized exact equality only; no semantic alias equivalence. This is the audit lens.
- SEMANTIC: `ai-shadow-issuer-semantic-identity-v1` only. EXACT and EQUIVALENT match; DISTINCT, AMBIGUOUS, and collisions do not. This is the primary Phase 00 lens.

Every issuer comparison is accounted as EXACT, EQUIVALENT, DISTINCT, or AMBIGUOUS. Semantic successes depending on at least one EQUIVALENT outcome are separately counted for mappings and complete attributions. Normalization cannot silently inflate results.

The freeze defines final validity, whole mapping, DIRECT precision/recall, semantic false-DIRECT, PROXY precision/recall, UNRESOLVED correctness, complete typed attribution, completeness, every role, principal proxy accuracy, policy false positives/negatives, incorrect ticker resolutions, identity outcomes, equivalent-dependent successes, corrective retry measures, and safety/persistence violations. C1E3 denominators are explicitly not reused.

Corrective retry reporting is frozen as first-pass validity, retry count/rate, validation-reason histogram, final validity, and semantic change after retry where determinable. Corrective retry is explicitly not repeatability.

Generalization primary gates are:

- final validity >= 98%;
- semantic identity-normalized DIRECT precision >= 95%;
- semantic identity-normalized DIRECT recall >= 90%;
- semantic false-DIRECT <= 5% of all frozen cases;
- zero incorrect deterministic ticker resolutions;
- zero safety/persistence violations.
