## Scope and independence

This package freezes datasets and offline validation design only. It does not implement C1E remediation, change the C1D guard, change the Luna prompt, change the canonical output schema, execute model inference, contact a provider, mutate a database, or mutate trading state.

Both v2 corpora were constructed before C1E remediation design and without inspecting detailed C1D3 case-level findings, observed C1D2 model outputs, expected answers derived from those outputs, or detailed C1D2 failure analysis. Construction used the frozen `event-asset-resolution-v1` policy, canonical `EventInput`, current manifest and lock formats, current benchmark contract, and the requested broad causal-boundary class distribution.

The original frozen 48, Generalization v1, and Boundary v1 remain unchanged. They are used only as prior corpora for leakage checks.

## Adjudication methodology

Each case is adjudicated from receipt-time evidence under the unchanged `event-asset-resolution-v1` policy:

- `DIRECT`: exactly one traded issuer is the principal causally attributable subject or affected issuer. Market relevance, materiality, event strength, and issuer familiarity are not eligibility gates.
- `PROXY`: there is no directly attributable principal traded issuer and exactly one bounded principal exposure is justified by the frozen policy.
- `UNRESOLVED`: neither one causal direct issuer nor one principal bounded exposure can be selected confidently. This includes multiple equally causal issuers, multiple equally plausible supported proxies, ambiguous issuer identity, incidental entity extraction, and merely contextual, comparative, commenting, or speculative-beneficiary mentions.

Expected deterministic resolution is adjudicated separately. A confident `DIRECT` label for an issuer outside the bounded resolver remains `DIRECT` with `expected_resolution_status: unresolved`. Supported proxy labels resolve. The Google/Alphabet case is `DIRECT` at causal attribution but explicitly `ambiguous` at deterministic resolution because the frozen policy cannot select a listed Alphabet share class.

No future causal-role, equal-causality, or principal-exposure field was added to the expected model-output contract. The manifests strictly decode through the current `DiagnosticManifest`, `EventInput`, and `DiagnosticLabel` types, so they can evaluate the current ten-field contract. Their case design and rationales remain suitable for a later typed contract without precommitting to that implementation.

## Independence and leakage evidence

`internal/modules/aishadow/holdout_datasets_test.go` validates all five frozen corpora: original 48, Generalization v1, Boundary v1, Generalization v2, and Boundary v2. It rejects duplicate IDs, duplicate canonical `EventInput` fingerprints, duplicate normalized title/summary wording, and token-set near-copies at a Jaccard threshold of `0.72`.

Final maximum cross-dataset normalized token-set similarity: `0.364`, between `issuer-generalization-v2-047` and `issuer-boundary-v2-027`, below the `0.72` rejection threshold. The pair is not a semantic copy: one tests an invalid oil-proxy lexical trigger in an oilseed levy; the other tests incidental extraction of Apple as a fruit crop. Manual review also replaced superficially reusable comparator, commenter, equal-pipeline, and cross-asset scenarios before final hashing. No duplicate ID, canonical input, normalized wording, trivial entity substitution, or strong semantic near-copy was retained.
