## Adjudication method

Each case was adjudicated in two independent layers:

1. Determine semantic mapping under the frozen policy: exactly one principal
   traded issuer is `DIRECT`; no eligible direct issuer plus exactly one
   bounded principal exposure is `PROXY`; otherwise abstain as `UNRESOLVED`.
2. Apply `event-asset-resolution-v1` to the adjudicated issuer or proxy and
   freeze the actual deterministic status separately as `resolved`,
   `ambiguous`, or `unresolved`.

Relevance and materiality were varied inside mapping classes and were never
used as class shortcuts. Multiple entity types, historical context,
commentators, beneficiaries, victims, suppliers, customers, divisions,
brands, legal entities, ticker-like strings, and market exposures were
adjudicated by causal role rather than presence.

### Issuer identity

A `DIRECT` label requires an exact intended legal issuer supported by the
event evidence. A brand or division maps to a parent only when the input
expressly supplies that legal relationship. Unsupported but exactly
identifiable traded issuers remain semantic `DIRECT` and receive deterministic
`unresolved`. Alphabet remains exact at issuer level while its security
resolution is `ambiguous` because the frozen resolver cannot choose a share
class. Same-name entities, brand-only reports, unidentified divisions, and
ticker-like non-company strings abstain rather than guessing.

### Proxy selection

A `PROXY` case has no eligible principal issuer and one supported principal
exposure from the existing resolver vocabulary. Proxy-negative cases include
secondary input costs, speculative beneficiaries, sector words embedded in
company names, equal co-principal exposures, and non-financial keyword
collisions. Presence of `oil`, `gold`, an index name, or a sector term is never
sufficient by itself.

### Typed-label sidecar decision

No new typed-attribution sidecar was created. The normal benchmark mapping,
exact issuer/proxy expectation, resolver status, and rationale are frozen now.
Typed role sidecars are deferred because C1F may narrowly revise the typed
contract or policy; freezing them in C1F0 would couple the unseen corpus to a
remediation design that does not yet exist. No future field was invented.

## Policy ambiguities retained

- Exact issuer recognition and deterministic resolver coverage remain separate.
- Alphabet issuer identity does not resolve the listed share-class choice.
- Brand or division evidence without a legal-parent link is insufficient.
- Equal principal issuers and equal principal proxies require abstention.
- A private or unidentified causal entity does not justify promoting a listed
  customer, supplier, beneficiary, or sector proxy.
- Typed-role expectations remain deliberately unfrozen until the C1F contract
  and policy are decided.
