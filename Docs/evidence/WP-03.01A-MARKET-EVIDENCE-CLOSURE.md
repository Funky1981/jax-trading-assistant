## OBSERVEDAT SEMANTICS

Previous behavior: a provider date such as `2026-08-25` became a UTC interval `[2026-08-25T00:00:00Z, 2026-08-26T00:00:00Z)` and canonical Observation `ObservedAt` used the interval end. `CanonicalMarketBar` documented this, but a caller could overlook the qualifier.

New behavior: `CanonicalMarketBar` now carries `TimestampAuthority=INTERVAL_BOUNDARY_ONLY`. `ExchangeCloseTime()` always fails closed for this provider-date representation. The provider date, UTC interval, `TimestampSemantics`, `Session=PROVIDER_EOD_SESSION_UNSPECIFIED`, and `MarketTimezone=UNKNOWN` remain explicit.

Why it cannot be interpreted as an authoritative exchange close: the supported provider-neutral projection has no successful exchange-close accessor; the only timestamp authority is an explicit interval-boundary enum. No exchange timezone, close time, or session is inferred.

Canonical fields: `ProviderDate`, `Start`, `End`, `TimestampSemantics`, `TimestampAuthority`, `Session`, `MarketTimezone`, `Adjustment`.

Research-facing behavior: consumers receive `CanonicalMarketBar`, not a vendor DTO, and must handle the explicit non-authoritative timestamp state.

Tests: `TestFinancialDatasetsHardenedDailyBarsEndToEnd` verifies interval construction and `ExchangeCloseTime()` failure.
