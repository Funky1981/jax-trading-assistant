# WP-00.03C1E1 typed causal-attribution contract architecture

Date: 2026-08-17

## Decision and scope

WP-00.03C1E1 is an architecture and contract-design package only. It does not
implement a schema, prompt, validator, policy, resolver, diagnostic profile, or
evaluation runner. It does not run inference or alter any dataset.

Decision:

- The accepted `ai-shadow-output-v4-issuer-resolution` contract is inadequate
  for deterministic causal-attribution policy and must remain immutable.
- Create a new contract identity, `ai-shadow-output-v5-causal-attribution`, in
  C1E2.
- Retain the ten v4 fields in v5 and add exactly two required typed fields:
  `issuer_attributions` and `principal_proxy_candidates`.
- Represent issuer causality per named issuer, because a selected-issuer-only
  field cannot preserve the distinction between principal, equally principal,
  secondary affected, contextual, and merely possible principal issuers.
- Represent proxy ambiguity as a bounded list of principal proxy candidates.
  Zero, one, and more-than-one entries encode none, unique, and
  multiple/ambiguous without a redundant proxy-cardinality field.
- Market relevance/materiality remains independent of causal attribution and
  must not gate DIRECT, PROXY, or UNRESOLVED.
- Deterministic Jax validates the typed relationships and enforces selection
  policy. It does not reconstruct causality from event prose or `reason`.
- `ai-shadow-causal-consistency-c1d-v1` is historical-only and is not eligible
  to remain active for the v5 path.
- GPT-5.6 Luna remains the selected hosted development model. No other model is
  proposed.

Phase 00 remains NO-GO. C1D v1 remains rejected for promotion.

## Reproduced architecture finding

The evidence validates, rather than merely assumes, the C1D3 conclusion:
market relevance/materiality is not causal attribution.

### Relevance causes false negatives

On Generalization v1, the raw Luna output was semantically correct on 45 of 48
cases. C1D v1 abstained 12 times: it removed two false DIRECT mappings but also
suppressed ten raw-correct mappings. On Boundary v1, raw Luna was semantically
correct on all 24 cases, while C1D v1 abstained four times and all four were
raw-correct mappings. Across these two development sets, C1D v1 therefore
suppressed 14 correct mappings while removing two incorrect mappings.

Concrete raw-correct DIRECT classifications suppressed solely because
`market_relevance` was not HIGH include:

- Generalization v1 case 001: Netflix was the sole issuer undertaking an
  operational expansion; Luna returned DIRECT with MEDIUM relevance.
- Generalization v1 cases 045 and 046: CrowdStrike and Lululemon each performed
  an explicit issuer-specific action; Luna returned DIRECT with MEDIUM
  relevance, and both identities were intentionally unsupported by the
  resolver.
- Boundary v1 case 007: Microsoft offered a limited adapter replacement; Luna
  returned DIRECT with MEDIUM relevance.
- Boundary v1 cases 020 through 022: a small Deckers annex, a minor Google
  operating-hours change, and a brief Exxon inspection were explicit causal
  issuer events with non-HIGH relevance.

Limited materiality changes neither who acted nor who was directly affected.

### Relevance can remove some false positives but does not type why

Generalization v1 cases 040 and 041 were false DIRECT mappings for Chevron and
Apple. LOW relevance let C1D v1 suppress them, but the same rule also suppressed
the genuine cases above. The relevance value identifies low materiality, not
whether an issuer is a principal subject, secondary affected party, possible
subject, or incidental mention.

### v4 prose often contains the missing distinction, but policy cannot use it

In accepted C1B output, Luna's structured `reason` frequently stated the right
causal fact while the selected mapping remained wrong:

- Original case 040 was returned as DIRECT/Amazon even though the reason said
  Amazon was only a size comparison and had no operational impact.
- Original case 041 was returned as DIRECT/NVIDIA even though the reason said
  the mention was only an acknowledgement and not a catalyst.
- Original case 021 was returned as a semiconductor PROXY even though the
  reason said NVIDIA and AMD had equal weight and no single issuer could be
  selected.

Parsing those sentences in Go would make deterministic policy reconstruct
natural-language causality. Leaving them as prose makes the evidence
unauditable and unenforceable. The contract must expose the distinction as
typed data.

### Entity counting is not causal attribution

Generalization v1 case 022 named Exxon Mobil and Chevron as equally causal joint
partners. Luna returned `PROXY/OIL_CATEGORY`; the C1D guard counted two
recognized issuers but did not abstain because the event category was
`energy_company`, not the guard's literal `company` category. More importantly,
even a robust entity count could not distinguish this case from the following
correct single-principal patterns:

- Visa subject to an order while Mastercard is only a comparator;
- NVIDIA affected by a supplier delay while AMD is explicitly unaffected;
- Amazon closing a centre while eBay only comments on sector demand.

The missing information is each entity's causal role, not its presence,
resolver recognition, or the event's category.

## Why the ten existing fields cannot be safely reinterpreted

The answer to contract question 1 is **no**.

The ten fields expose a selected mapping, relevance, confidence, horizon,
direction, catalyst label, prose reason, and missing-evidence prose. They do not
expose the causal basis needed to validate that selection:

- `market_relevance` measures likely market materiality. It is independent of
  causal subject identity.
- `mapping_status` says which of DIRECT, PROXY, or UNRESOLVED the model selected.
  It does not say whether there was one principal issuer, multiple equal
  issuers, an uncertain issuer, or no issuer subject.
- `direct_issuer` has capacity for one string only. It cannot retain other named
  issuers or their roles.
- `proxy_exposure` has capacity for one chosen exposure only. It cannot show
  that two exposures were plausible, so a questionable choice is
  indistinguishable from a unique one.
- `mapping_confidence` describes confidence in the selected single mapping. It
  cannot encode causal role or cardinality. An UNRESOLVED mapping is required to
  be LOW even when the model is highly certain that two issuers are equally
  causal.
- `reason` and `missing_evidence` are audit prose, not policy inputs. Combining
  or parsing them would move language interpretation into Go.

Reinterpreting `market_relevance` as causal confidence would silently change a
historical metric and recreate C1D's error. Reinterpreting `direct_issuer` as a
comma-separated list would destroy identity boundaries and strict validation.
Reinterpreting `proxy_exposure` or `NONE` as an ambiguity sentinel would erase
the distinction between no proxy and multiple proxy candidates. None is safe.

## Candidate designs considered

### A. More top-level fields about the selected mapping

Examples include `causal_role`, `principal_issuer_count`, or
`principal_exposure_status` attached only to `direct_issuer` or
`proxy_exposure`.

This is compact, but rejected. It cannot preserve the role of non-selected
named issuers. A DIRECT/Visa result would still not record whether Mastercard
was contextual, secondary affected, or an equal principal incorrectly
discarded. Counts also do not identify which issuer occupies which role.

### B. Per-entity attribution only

An array of `{issuer, causal_role}` objects correctly represents principal,
equal, secondary, contextual, and uncertain issuers. It is necessary for issuer
auditability.

Used alone, however, it does not describe whether there is zero, one, or more
than one defensible bounded proxy exposure. Extending the same object model to
all abstract exposures would add unnecessary entity/exposure union types.

### C. Large nested causal graph

A general graph containing subjects, actions, beneficiaries, victims,
commentators, evidence spans, edge confidence, and exposure nodes would be
expressive. It is rejected as disproportionate to Phase 00 mapping policy. It
would increase output tokens, schema complexity, labeling cost, and failure
modes while asking Luna to emit facts the resolver does not consume.

### D. Compact hybrid: selected design

Use per-issuer roles plus a bounded list of principal proxy candidates. Keep the
existing selected mapping fields as a declared projection for continuity and
raw-metric compatibility.

This is the minimum design that preserves the distinctions demonstrated by the
evidence. It adds no causal graph, evidence-span extraction, confidence score,
entity type hierarchy, or separate cardinality flags.

## Selected v5 model-output contract

The new output contract identity is:

`ai-shadow-output-v5-causal-attribution`

The v5 object retains all ten v4 fields unchanged in wire name and basic type,
then adds these two required fields:

| Field | Type | Required constraints | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `issuer_attributions` | array of objects | 0 to 10 items; each object has exactly `issuer` and `causal_role`; application validator rejects duplicate normalized identities | Every explicitly named traded-issuer identity or plausible traded-issuer identity relevant to selection, including non-selected issuer mentions |
| `principal_proxy_candidates` | array of strings | 0 to the active bounded exposure count; every item is an active proxy-exposure enum value excluding `NONE`; application validator rejects duplicates | Bounded exposures that remain plausible as the principal mapping when there is no principal direct issuer |

Each `issuer_attributions` item has:

| Field | Type | Constraints |
|---|---|---|
| `issuer` | string | trimmed length 1 to 200; issuer name only, never a ticker |
| `causal_role` | enum string | one of the five values below |

`causal_role` enum:

- `PRINCIPAL`: the one issuer that is the principal causal event subject.
- `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL`: one of two or more issuers with equal principal causality;
  selecting one would be arbitrary.
- `SECONDARY_AFFECTED`: an issuer that may benefit or suffer from the event but
  is not the principal event subject. This role does not license DIRECT.
- `CONTEXT_ONLY`: a commentator, comparator, venue, landmark, product mention,
  historical reference, or other issuer mention with no principal causal role.
- `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL`: the evidence makes this issuer a plausible principal
  but does not establish it unambiguously.

The schema-enforced shape is equivalent to this specimen; the exact exposure
enum and array maximum remain resolver-derived:

```json
{
  "market_relevance": "MEDIUM",
  "mapping_status": "DIRECT",
  "direct_issuer": "Example Holdings",
  "proxy_exposure": "NONE",
  "mapping_confidence": "HIGH",
  "expected_horizon": "MULTI_DAY",
  "likely_direction": "POSITIVE",
  "catalyst_type": "company action",
  "reason": "The named issuer is the unique principal subject; another named company only supplied context.",
  "missing_evidence": [],
  "issuer_attributions": [
    {"issuer": "Example Holdings", "causal_role": "PRINCIPAL"},
    {"issuer": "Comparison Corp", "causal_role": "CONTEXT_ONLY"}
  ],
  "principal_proxy_candidates": []
}
```

The two arrays deliberately encode cardinality rather than duplicating it:

- one `PRINCIPAL` means exactly one principal issuer;
- two or more `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL` items mean equal/multiple causality;
- one or more `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL` items mean issuer attribution is ambiguous;
- no principal/equal/possible role means no directly attributable principal
  issuer, even if secondary or contextual issuers are present;
- zero proxy candidates means no supported principal proxy;
- one proxy candidate means one unique principal proxy exposure;
- two or more proxy candidates mean multiple/ambiguous proxy exposures.

## Required versus redundant causal concepts

Required:

- typed per-issuer causal role;
- explicit equal-principal and possible-principal roles;
- preservation of secondary affected and context-only issuer mentions;
- bounded principal proxy candidates with observable cardinality.

Redundant or unnecessary for C1E:

- a separate `principal_causal_subject` field: `PRINCIPAL` plus
  `direct_issuer` already supplies it;
- `issuer_cardinality` or `equal_causality` booleans: roles and array counts
  derive both deterministically;
- `principal_proxy_exposure` in addition to the existing `proxy_exposure`: a
  one-item candidate array and equality invariant supply the typed basis;
- `proxy_cardinality`: candidate-array length supplies it;
- `causal_confidence`: the existing categorical `mapping_confidence` remains
  confidence in the selected mapping, while `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL` and multiple
  proxy candidates express attribution uncertainty directly;
- `causal_evidence_status`: roles, candidate cardinality, `reason`, and
  `missing_evidence` already distinguish a usable selection from ambiguity;
- evidence quotations or character spans: useful for a future evidence model,
  but not required for deterministic Phase 00 selection and costly to label;
- beneficiary-versus-victim polarity: `SECONDARY_AFFECTED` is the policy-relevant
  distinction. Directional sign for a non-selected issuer is outside this
  contract's single-mapping purpose.

## Representation of required situations

| Situation | Typed representation | Declared mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Exactly one principal issuer | exactly one `PRINCIPAL`; no proxy candidates | DIRECT with matching `direct_issuer` |
| Several named issuers, one principal | one `PRINCIPAL`; all others `SECONDARY_AFFECTED` or `CONTEXT_ONLY` as appropriate | DIRECT for the principal |
| Several equally causal issuers | at least two `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL`; no `PRINCIPAL`, `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL`, or proxy candidates | UNRESOLVED |
| Merely mentioned issuer | `CONTEXT_ONLY` | does not by itself determine mapping |
| Indirect beneficiary or victim | `SECONDARY_AFFECTED` | does not by itself license DIRECT |
| Commentator, comparator, or venue issuer | `CONTEXT_ONLY` | does not by itself license DIRECT |
| Unknown-to-policy but causally identifiable traded issuer | one `PRINCIPAL` using the explicit issuer name | DIRECT; resolver may return unresolved |
| Causal issuer identity is plausible but not established | one or more `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL` | UNRESOLVED |
| Legitimate proxy with no direct principal issuer | no principal/equal/possible issuer; exactly one proxy candidate | PROXY with matching `proxy_exposure` |
| Multiple plausible proxies | no principal/equal/possible issuer; at least two proxy candidates | UNRESOLVED |
| No direct issuer and no supported proxy | no principal/equal/possible issuer; zero proxy candidates | UNRESOLVED |

## Validator invariants

Schema validation must continue to require a closed root object, all fields,
closed attribution-item objects, bounded strings and arrays, and enum-only
values. Application validation must enforce relationships that JSON Schema
cannot express safely in the supported strict subset.

### General attribution invariants

1. Issuer names are non-empty, contain no ticker field, and are unique after the
   existing deterministic issuer-name normalization.
2. At most one item has role `PRINCIPAL`.
3. `PRINCIPAL` cannot coexist with `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL` or
   `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL`.
4. `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL` occurs either zero times or at least twice.
5. `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL` cannot coexist with `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL`.
6. `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL` occurs at least once when used and cannot coexist with
   `PRINCIPAL` or `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL`.
7. `SECONDARY_AFFECTED` and `CONTEXT_ONLY` may coexist with one `PRINCIPAL`,
   multiple `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL`, one or more `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL`, or neither.
   They never create selection eligibility.
8. Proxy candidates are active bounded exposure keys, exclude `NONE`, and are
   unique. Tickers remain unavailable to the model.
9. Any `PRINCIPAL`, `EQUAL_PRINCIPAL`, or `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL` role requires an
   empty proxy-candidate array. A proxy must never substitute for a known,
   equal, or ambiguous direct-issuer causal situation.

### Mapping projection invariants

10. DIRECT requires exactly one `PRINCIPAL`, zero equal/possible roles, an empty
    proxy-candidate array, non-empty `direct_issuer`, and
    `proxy_exposure=NONE`.
11. The DIRECT `direct_issuer` must equal the sole `PRINCIPAL.issuer` after
    trimming only. Jax does not choose among or semantically reconcile model
    names.
12. PROXY requires no principal/equal/possible issuer, exactly one proxy
    candidate, an empty `direct_issuer`, and `proxy_exposure` equal to that
    candidate.
13. UNRESOLVED requires empty `direct_issuer` and `proxy_exposure=NONE`, and one
    of: multiple equal principals, possible principal ambiguity, no principal
    and no proxy candidate, or no principal and multiple proxy candidates.
14. A unique principal issuer must be declared DIRECT even when the resolver
    does not know the issuer. Resolver coverage is not causal classification.
15. A unique proxy candidate must be declared PROXY only when no direct
    principal/equal/possible issuer exists.

### Existing-field invariants retained

16. DIRECT retains HIGH or MEDIUM `mapping_confidence` and valid receipt-time
    anchors.
17. PROXY retains the v4 confidence allowance, including LOW when the one
    exposure is weak but still defensible.
18. UNRESOLVED retains LOW `mapping_confidence`. This describes confidence in a
    selectable single mapping, not confidence that equal causality exists.
19. `market_relevance` has no validation or policy coupling to mapping status,
    role, candidate count, confidence, or resolver outcome.
20. Horizon, direction, and catalyst fields receive no new causal coupling.

An invalid cross-field combination receives the existing bounded corrective
retry. A final invalid response remains invalid and must not be silently
coerced to another semantic label. Resolution is not invoked for invalid output.

## Deterministic Jax policy

The new policy identity is:

`ai-shadow-causal-attribution-c1e-v1`

The resolver policy remains:

`event-asset-resolution-v1`

After structural and semantic validation, the attribution policy derives the
only permissible effective mapping from typed roles and candidate cardinality:

1. **Accept DIRECT** when there is exactly one `PRINCIPAL`. Pass that exact
   issuer string to `ResolveIssuer`. Do not require resolver coverage and do
   not fall back to a proxy if resolution is unresolved or ambiguous.
2. **Accept PROXY** when there is no principal, equal-principal, or
   possible-principal issuer and there is exactly one bounded principal proxy
   candidate. Pass that exact exposure to `ResolveProxyExposure`.
3. **Abstain UNRESOLVED** for multiple equal principals, possible-principal
   ambiguity, no principal/no proxy, or multiple proxy candidates.
4. **Fail closed** for an impossible state or projection mismatch. The policy
   never upgrades UNRESOLVED, substitutes an issuer/exposure, selects one equal
   principal, selects one of multiple proxies, or parses `reason`.

The declared `mapping_status`, `direct_issuer`, and `proxy_exposure` remain raw
model-output fields for audit and historical metric continuity. They must match
the typed derivation to pass validation. The persisted policy decision remains
separate from raw model output and deterministic resolver output.

Recommended future policy reason codes are typed and non-lexical:

- `UNIQUE_PRINCIPAL_ISSUER_ACCEPTED`
- `UNIQUE_PRINCIPAL_PROXY_ACCEPTED`
- `MULTIPLE_EQUAL_PRINCIPAL_ISSUERS`
- `POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL_ISSUER`
- `NO_PRINCIPAL_MAPPING`
- `MULTIPLE_PRINCIPAL_PROXY_CANDIDATES`
- `CAUSAL_ATTRIBUTION_PROJECTION_MISMATCH`

The last code is a fail-closed invariant breach, not a normal accepted result.

## Prompt impact

The new prompt identity is:

`ai-shadow-prompt-v5-causal-attribution`

C1E2 should make the smallest semantic prompt change that teaches Luna the new
fields. It should not tune against individual cases.

Schema-enforced requirements:

- both new fields are always present;
- attribution objects have exactly two fields;
- causal roles and proxy candidates are enums;
- all strings and arrays are bounded;
- tickers/symbol fields and unknown fields remain impossible.

Semantic instructions Luna must understand:

- causal subject identity is independent of market relevance/materiality;
- name presence alone is not DIRECT;
- distinguish the one principal issuer from secondary affected and
  context-only issuers;
- mark equivalent co-principals explicitly rather than selecting one or
  substituting a sector proxy;
- use possible-principal only when receipt-time evidence cannot establish the
  issuer relationship;
- preserve an explicit unknown-to-resolver traded issuer as PRINCIPAL/DIRECT;
- populate principal proxy candidates only when no direct principal, equal, or
  possible principal issuer exists;
- return all defensible principal proxy candidates rather than arbitrarily
  choosing one;
- make the existing selected mapping fields the exact projection of the typed
  causal fields.

Generic role definitions and one neutral shape example are sufficient. The
final prompt text is a C1E2 implementation artifact, not part of C1E1.

   identity and new evaluation cell; v2 then becomes development evidence for
   that later version.
8. Measure semantic repeatability only with a predeclared repeated-run profile
   and separate authorization. Do not blend it into a single-run metric.

### Metrics

Historical metric names and values remain unchanged. C1E3 reports new metrics
under explicit RAW and EFFECTIVE scopes:

- schema validity, first-pass validity, final validity, and retry count;
- RAW and EFFECTIVE DIRECT precision/recall and false DIRECT;
- RAW and EFFECTIVE PROXY precision/recall and false PROXY;
- RAW and EFFECTIVE UNRESOLVED correctness;
- whole-case causal-attribution exact match;
- principal-issuer correctness: exact normalized set and correct unique/equal
  role;
- equal-causality correctness: correct detection plus exact equal-principal set;
- secondary/context role correctness for named issuer candidates;
- principal-proxy correctness: exact bounded candidate set and correct
  zero/one/multiple cardinality;
- deterministic attribution-policy correctness;
- deterministic resolver correctness only after a semantically correct,
  policy-accepted DIRECT or PROXY input;
- policy-induced false negatives: raw-correct selectable mappings changed to
  UNRESOLVED;
- policy-induced false positives, if any;
- semantic repeatability by typed role set, candidate set, raw mapping, and
  effective outcome in a later repeated profile.

Whole-case causal-attribution exact match is strict. Component metrics are also
reported so one contextual-role error does not obscure correct principal
selection. Issuer comparison uses the deterministic normalization already used
for audit, not fuzzy matching. No metric treats coincidentally identical tickers
from different proxy exposures as semantic agreement.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Role completeness is model-semantic, not schema-provable.** The schema can
  require shape but cannot prove Luna listed every issuer mention. Mitigate with
  explicit prompt semantics, development cases, typed-label scoring, and exact
  whole-case metrics; do not add lexical Go inference.
- **Additional fields can reduce first-pass validity or increase output size.**
  The design adds two compact arrays and no evidence quotations or duplicated
  status fields. Correctness and auditability take precedence over a small
  token saving.
- **`POSSIBLE_PRINCIPAL` may be overused as an abstention shortcut.** Measure
  DIRECT/PROXY recall and policy-induced false negatives, and retain role-level
  scoring.
- **Context versus secondary affected can be semantically subtle.** Both are
  non-selecting roles, but they remain separate for audit. Component and
  effective-outcome metrics should be reported separately.
- **Existing `mapping_confidence` can be misunderstood for confidently equal
  causality.** Document that it concerns selection of one mapping; equality is
  represented by typed roles.
- **Frozen v2 labels may not already contain the new fields.** Freeze a separate
  C1E3 typed-label sidecar before inference output is viewed; never modify the
  frozen datasets or adjudicate after seeing model answers.
- **Historical report counters have previously conflated guard abstention,
  resolver coverage, and resolution correctness.** New metrics must preserve
  raw semantic, attribution policy, and resolver stages independently.
- **C1D remains available in code for replay.** Fail-closed profile/version
  compatibility must prevent accidental v5 activation.
- **Ticker-token diagnostics remain lexically noisy.** Keep the issue in a
  separate work package so it cannot distort causal-contract design.
