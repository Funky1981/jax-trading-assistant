## Docker-backed validation update - 2026-06-04

- `scripts/migrate.ps1 up` with `DATABASE_URL=postgresql://jax:jax@localhost:5433/jax?sslmode=disable` initially exposed a migration 29 PostgreSQL syntax failure because `sentiment_aggregates.window` used the reserved word `window`.
