# VAL-03R1 Independent Recovery Options

This is a non-outcome design record. No 2025 or 2026 data was accessed and no
option is selected here.

## Shared constraints

- `ma_crossover_v1` remains unresolved.
- A new boundary would require a new externally authorized preregistration and
  execution identity.
- The contaminated 2023–2024 run cannot be repaired or rerun under its old
  identity.
- Existing effective-block floor: at least 12 non-empty instrument-year
  blocks.
- Fixed universe: 9 instruments.
- No provider fallback is assumed.

## Option A — 2025 only

- Coverage: one calendar year, subject to a separately authorized acquisition.
- Theoretical maximum instrument-year blocks: 9.
- Existing 12-block floor: structurally impossible in a single 2025-only year.
- Requirements: new sealed-boundary decision, complete adjusted/raw data
  contract and fresh execution freeze.
- Contamination: must remain separate from the contaminated 2023–2024 run.
- Holdout consequence: 2025 would no longer remain the current sealed holdout.
- Conclusion: cannot satisfy the existing block floor by itself.

## Option B — 2025 plus a separately frozen later period

- Coverage: 2025 and one or more later calendar years, if available and
  independently authorized.
- Theoretical maximum blocks: 9 per calendar year; at least two years are
  required even before accounting for empty blocks.
- Requirements: future data entitlement, point-in-time provenance, explicit
  partitioning and a new preregistration identity.
- Contamination: later periods must not be selected based on observed outcomes;
  the contaminated 2023–2024 run remains excluded.
- Holdout consequence: the current 2025 seal would need an owner/technical-lead
  decision before any access.
- Conclusion: structurally capable of reaching 12 blocks, subject to actual
  coverage and independent authorization.

## Option C — Another independent available boundary

- Coverage: only a genuinely independent period or dataset boundary that can be
  proven available under the approved provider and provenance contract.
- Theoretical maximum blocks: 9 times the number of qualifying calendar years.
- Requirements: no new provider shopping, complete data-quality evidence,
  independent contamination review and a new frozen execution identity.
- Contamination: boundary must not overlap or depend on the invalid 2023–2024
  execution.
- Holdout consequence: current 2025 seal remains intact unless separately
  authorized otherwise.
- Conclusion: methodological option only; feasibility is unknown until a
  future authorized preflight.

## External decision required

The technical lead must choose whether any option is scientifically and
operationally justified. VAL-03R1 does not acquire data, inspect outcomes or
authorize a new OOS run.
