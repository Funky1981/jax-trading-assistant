# WP-10.06 implementation evidence

Status: `IMPLEMENTED / INTERNALLY VERIFIED`

Crash/recovery handling uses explicit `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`. Recovery does
not assume an uncertain operation succeeded and cannot auto-advance to human
approval or paper intent. Only an operator can resolve reconciliation, and the
safe outcomes are cancellation or blocking.

`DurableSnapshot` preserves workflows, complete audit streams, idempotency
records, paper intents and breaker state. Restore validates the snapshot
version, identities, content-addressed audit events, sequence/state agreement,
paper-intent pairing and breaker contracts before accepting state. Corruption,
unsupported versions and missing artifacts fail closed.
