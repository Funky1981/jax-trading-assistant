## Objective
Deliver **Canonical portfolio/position/account state** as the next smallest independently reviewable increment of Phase 09.

## Implementation evidence

- Status: implemented and internally verified; WP-09.02 current.
- Canonical boundary: `internal/modules/portfoliorisk/state.go`.
- Existing-code classification: `libs/contracts/domain/position.go` is legacy
  display/account vocabulary with zero-valued fields; `internal/decisioning/risk`
  is reusable safety vocabulary but not a canonical observed-state contract;
  `internal/modules/quant` is current deterministic arithmetic and will be
  reused for compatible analytics. Historical broker/trade paths are unsafe for
  this phase and remain untouched.
- `PortfolioSnapshot` separates known values from unknown values, carries
  provider, source, timestamp, currency, synthetic status, valuation basis and
  provenance, and receives a stable SHA-256 identity from canonical JSON.
- Snapshots are append-only in memory and in the additive Postgres
  `portfolio_snapshots` migration. They contain observed facts only; no account,
  cash, position, order, trade or fill mutation is performed.
- Freshness returns `FRESH`, `STALE` or `UNKNOWN`; missing cash/price/value,
  unresolved instruments, unsupported currencies and stale valuations fail
  closed for later risk evaluation.
- Phase-09 mechanics use explicitly labelled synthetic fixtures. No real
  portfolio or broker integration is claimed, and no credential was added.
- Verification: `go test ./internal/modules/portfoliorisk -count=1`.
