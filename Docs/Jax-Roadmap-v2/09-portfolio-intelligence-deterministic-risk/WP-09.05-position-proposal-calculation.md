## Objective

## Implementation evidence

- Status: implemented and internally verified; WP-09.06 current.
- `CalculatePositionProposal` is descriptive risk arithmetic using an explicit
  equity risk fraction, entry/stop unit risk, maximum position/concentration
  caps and known cash. It preserves fractional quantity semantics because no
  venue lot rules are available, rather than fabricating executable precision.
- Unknown/stale cash, equity, portfolio, analytics, policy, currency or sizing
  inputs fail closed. Insufficient known cash cannot be silently spent.
- The result contains no order, route, venue, TIF, broker or execution fields;
  it records `ExecutionAuthority=NONE` and `PortfolioMutated=false`. The
  proposal is not applied to the canonical portfolio.
- Verification: `go test ./internal/modules/portfoliorisk -count=1`.
